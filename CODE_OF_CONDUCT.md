# Code of Conduct

SimpleSCP welcomes constructive participation from users and contributors.

Be respectful, specific, and focused on the work. Technical disagreement is fine; personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not.

When reporting bugs or reviewing code, critique the behavior, design, or implementation rather than the person who created it.

Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, security-sensitive data, or non-public infrastructure details.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not used as leverage in public discussions.
